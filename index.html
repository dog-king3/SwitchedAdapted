Switched Adapted Toys Mission

Many children with disabilities face barriers when playing with ordinary toys. Small buttons, fine-motor requirements, or multiple controls can make a toy difficult or impossible to use.

Switched Adapted Toys removes those barriers by modifying toys so they can be activated with an accessible external switch — giving children the independence, confidence, and joy they deserve.
