Switch on the Joy

Making play more accessible for every child.

We adapt toys so children with disabilities can interact with them using an accessible external switch.

Learn more See last year's event
Real toys. Real inclusion.

We create playful solutions that remove barriers.

Many children with disabilities face barriers when they try to interact with everyday toys. Small buttons, fine-motor demands, and complicated controls can make play harder than it should be.

Switched Adapted Toys changes that by modifying toys so they can be activated with an accessible external switch. The result is more independence, more confidence, and more joy.

Small switch. Big possibilities.

How It Works

1

Choose a toy

We identify a toy that can bring joy, curiosity, and meaningful play to a child.

2

Plan the adaptation

We look at the toy's controls and design a simple switch-based solution that works well.

3

Adapt the toy

We adjust the toy so it can be activated by an accessible external switch instead of a difficult control.

4

Test and refine

We check usability, comfort, and reliability so the result feels natural and dependable.

5

Play

The toy becomes more accessible, more independent, and full of fun for the child using it.
