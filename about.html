About Switch on the Joy

A community-focused project built around creative problem-solving and accessible play.

Switch on the Joy is a community-focused initiative that uses engineering, creativity, and teamwork to make play more accessible for children with disabilities.

The challenge

Many toys are designed around small buttons, fine-motor movements, or multiple controls. Those design choices can create barriers for children who cannot easily use them.

What the project explores

Switch on the Joy explores ways to adapt toys so they can be activated through accessible external switches. The goal is to make familiar play experiences reachable, engaging, and more independent.

Why it matters

Accessible play can create opportunities for children to interact, explore, learn, and participate more independently. It can also help build confidence, connection, and joyful everyday experiences.

Why We Do It

  • Engineering can solve real-world problems in ways that directly help people.
  • Accessibility is a meaningful design challenge, not an afterthought.
  • Students can use robotics and engineering skills outside competition to create community impact.
  • Teamwork, creativity, and problem-solving all matter when a project is centered on inclusion.
  • The project is designed to create meaningful, practical community impact and support more inclusive play opportunities.
