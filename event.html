Community impact

What the day focused on

The event centered on identifying toys that could be adapted for accessible external switches and thinking practically about how to improve play experiences for children with disabilities.

How the community came together

Volunteers shared ideas, worked through design challenges, and supported one another while testing adapted toys and learning from the process. The day showcased the value of engineering, creativity, and inclusive design in a real community setting.
